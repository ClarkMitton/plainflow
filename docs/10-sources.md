# Sources

The rules and the template were built against these. Verify against the live pages before relying on any detail; guidance moves.

## ICO

- When do we need to do a DPIA? https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/accountability-and-governance/data-protection-impact-assessments-dpias/when-do-we-need-to-do-a-dpia
  Screening is a high-level test for features pointing to high risk. Article 35(3) three automatic triggers, the ICO's own list (innovative tech, denial of service, large-scale profiling, biometrics, genetic, data matching, invisible processing, tracking, targeting children, risk of physical harm), and the European nine criteria. Two or more criteria as the rule of thumb. Good practice to do a DPIA for any major project involving personal data even when not required.
- How do we do a DPIA? (seven steps and the sample template) https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/accountability-and-governance/data-protection-impact-assessments-dpias/how-do-we-do-a-dpia
- How do you determine whether you are a controller or processor? https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/controllers-and-processors/controllers-and-processors/how-do-you-determine-whether-you-are-a-controller-or-processor
  An organisation is not by nature one or the other. The key question is who determines purposes and means. A processor acting outside instructions becomes a controller for that element. You cannot be both for the same processing activity.
- What needs to be included in the contract (Article 28) https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/accountability-and-governance/contracts-and-liabilities-between-controllers-and-processors-multi/what-needs-to-be-included-in-the-contract/
- Data sharing code of practice https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/data-sharing/data-sharing-a-code-of-practice/
- Guidance on AI and data protection https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/artificial-intelligence/
- Rights related to automated decision-making including profiling https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/individual-rights/individual-rights/rights-related-to-automated-decision-making-including-profiling/
- Children's code DPIA template (Annex D) https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/childrens-information/childrens-code-guidance-and-resources/age-appropriate-design-a-code-of-practice-for-online-services/annex-d-dpia-template/

## DfE

- Generative AI and data protection in schools https://www.gov.uk/guidance/data-protection-in-schools/generative-artificial-intelligence-ai-and-data-protection-in-schools
- Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 compliance checklist for schools https://www.gov.uk/guidance/data-protection-in-schools/data-use-and-access-act-2025-duaa-compliance-checklist-for-schools
  Automated decision-making: identify systems, ensure a person can review and change outcomes, explain in privacy notices, give a simple way to challenge and request human review. Children's data: build privacy in by default; complete DPIAs for AI, EdTech and monitoring. Recognised legitimate interests. Complaints procedure. SAR changes.
- Generative AI in education (policy paper) https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/generative-artificial-intelligence-in-education/generative-artificial-intelligence-ai-in-education
- Procuring EdTech https://www.gov.uk/guidance/data-protection-in-schools/procuring-educational-technology-edtech
  Understand the roles of school and supplier per feature; the supplier may be a controller for features parents sign up to separately.

## Templates reviewed

ICO sample DPIA template; The Key for School Leaders DPIA template; DN Colleges DPIA template; Enfield schools DPIA pre-screening form (the only one asking the controller/processor role per supplier); a Seesaw DPIA built on the ICO sample; Essex University data processor contract checklist. All are the ICO seven-step body with a screening front end. None has a per-company DPA section, a data flow diagram slot, or sub-processor recording, which is what this template adds.
